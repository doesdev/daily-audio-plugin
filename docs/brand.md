# The brand

The name is **Daily Audio Plugin**, written **DAP** where it is short.
It ships everywhere a plugin does, and it is said one way.

- A host shows the maker as `Daily Audio Plugin` (`kit::VENDOR`,
  which every plugin's `Info` uses). Hosts find plugins by their ids,
  which have not changed and never do, so a project made before the
  name still opens.
- In words, a plugin is "Undertow, a Daily Audio Plugin" once, and
  "DAP" after. "DAP plugin" says plugin twice: say "DAP instance" for
  one that is open, "a DAP effect" or "a DAP instrument" for a kind.
- The editor says it where the name is: pointing at a plugin's name
  says it is one of DAP's. Linking is offered between "DAP instances"
  because it works across all of them and nothing else.
- A manual and a release say it once, in their first line.
- The folder a person's presets live in is still `doesdev`
  (`kit::HOME`). It is the name the first releases used, and moving it
  would strand what people have saved.

## The mark

`brand/dap-dark.svg` is for a dark ground and `brand/dap-light.svg`
for a light one; `brand/dap.svg` takes its letters' colour from the
text round it. They are drawn from the owner's draft: the letters
and the line of words are traced from it, and the dots, dashes and
trails are placed by eye where it has them, in its three colours.
The draft's light version had a pair of chevrons in the P that its
dark version did not; they are left out until one way is chosen.

The mark is in the README and in every editor. There it sits at the
top left corner of the glass, 128 wide, with the plugin's name
centred under its letters in purple, at 15 px. On the dark theme it
breaks past the corner's edge; on the light theme, whose ground is
pale, it keeps inside the corner, since the part past the edge would
be cream on grey. The placement was chosen on the design canvas. A
plugin's one line about itself, which used to sit under its name, is
now what pointing at the name shows.
